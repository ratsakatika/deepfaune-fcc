Species: candidates and per-species overrides

Unticking a species removes it from the candidate set: those detections are re-scored and fall to their next-best species. This is the only place species exclusion belongs - the raw scores are recorded for every class, so the decision is reversible here and permanent if made during classification. Blank override cells use the global values above.

Scores are re-derived from the per-class score_* columns, so thresholds and species exclusions apply retrospectively to the whole archive without reclassifying anything. Events are grouped by sequence_id; if your CSV predates that column, the folder path and seqnum are used instead. Sequence length is the elapsed time across an event; the grouping itself was fixed by the maxlag used when the archive was classified.
